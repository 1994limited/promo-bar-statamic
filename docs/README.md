# Promo Bar documentation

Promo Bar shows announcement and promo bars at the top of your pages: scheduled, targeted, styled in the Control Panel, and safe behind static caching. Every template example has an Antlers and a Blade version.

## Setting up

1. [Installation](installation.md): requirements, installing, the scheduler, updating.
2. [Making bars](control-panel.md): Content → Promo Bars, every field, order and permissions.
3. [Styles](styles.md): the presets, editing them, a bar's own colours, and your own CSS.

## On your site

4. [Templates](templates.md): the tag, where to put it, one bar or several, and the parameters.
5. [Static caching](caching.md): how bars stay right on cached pages, and what clears them.
6. [Analytics](analytics.md): sending clicks, dismissals, copies and views to your tracker.
7. [Cargo](cargo.md): free delivery progress, and codes from Cargo's discounts.
8. [Accessibility](accessibility.md): what the markup and script do, and what to check in your design.

## Reference

9. [Configuration](configuration.md): `config/promo-bar.php` and the settings page.
10. [JavaScript](javascript.md): `window.PromoBar`, events, and the `data-promo-bar-*` attributes.
11. [PHP API](php-api.md): the `PromoBars` service, events, and storage drivers.
12. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): common problems and fixes.
13. [The demo shop](demo.md): Harbour Coffee, a site to try it on.
